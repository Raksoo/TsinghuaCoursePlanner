Data & sharing

Courses live in your browser only. The file itself carries no data — export your list as JSON to pass it on, or export to .ics to bring your schedule into another calendar app.

Sharing: send this HTML file (it runs offline, just double-click it). To take over your selection as well, the other person imports the exported JSON file.

Calendar import: the .ics file uses whichever statuses are currently shown on the week view (toggle them there first). On Android, open the downloaded file with the Google Calendar app, or import it into Google Calendar on desktop — it then syncs to your phone automatically.
